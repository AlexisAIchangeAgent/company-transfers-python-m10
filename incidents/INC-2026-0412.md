## Customer report (as told on the phone)

Customer: Tudor Vatavu, customer ID C-1002, retail segment.

"On 12 October I sent a standard transfer of EUR 123.01 to a supplier. The app showed an exchange rate of 19.8765. The fee on my statement is MDL 12.22.
My accountant checked it against your tariff and it does not match. I did the calculation
myself: 0.5% of EUR 123.01 is EUR 0.62, and EUR 0.62 at 19.8765 is MDL 12.32. So the fee is
wrong. I want to know how you compute it, and I want a correct statement for my books."

## What we know

- Transfer date: 12 October 2026.
- Transfer type: standard (the customer confirmed it was not instant).
- Amount: EUR 123.01.
- Exchange rate shown to the customer: 19.8765 (EUR to MDL).
- Fee charged: MDL 12.22 (visible in the CRM, transfer history of C-1002).
- Fee expected by the customer: MDL 12.32 (his own calculation, see above).

## What we do not know yet

- Time of the transfer: unknown. The customer does not remember, the statement shows the date only.
- Channel: unknown. The customer said "the app", then "maybe the website". Not confirmed.
- Whether other customers are affected: not checked.
- Whether the rate used by the fee service is the rate shown to the customer: not checked.

## Level 1 note

"Probably the exchange rate API was down on 12 October and the fee was computed with an old
rate. Several customers complained about slow payments that week. Suggest to refund the
difference (MDL 0.10) and close."

## Impact

- 212 EUR transfers were made by retail and premium customers in October 2026 (up to 13 October).
- Regulatory note: fees charged must match the published tariff (docs/fee-rules.md). Any
  systematic difference must be reported to Compliance, even if the amounts are small.

## Requested from level 2

1. Confirm or reject the difference, with the calculation.
2. Say whether the problem is on this transfer only or systematic.
3. Propose the next step (correction, customer answer, report to Compliance).
