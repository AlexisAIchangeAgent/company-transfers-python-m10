# KB-02 · Deadlines and extensions

Owner: DPO of Company Bank SA (invented). Version 1.3. Audience: privacy desk, DPO.

## The standard deadline
The bank answers within 30 calendar days from the receipt of the request. Day 1 is the day after
receipt. Weekends and public holidays count. Example: a request received on 31 January is due on
2 March in a year that is not a leap year.

## Extension
For a complex request, or many requests from the same person, the DPO may extend the deadline once,
by up to 60 more days. Only the DPO decides an extension. The requester is told within the first
30 days, with the reason.

## Clock and identity
The clock starts at receipt. When the identity is missing, the privacy desk still records the
receipt date, and the due date is recorded but not promised to the requester.
