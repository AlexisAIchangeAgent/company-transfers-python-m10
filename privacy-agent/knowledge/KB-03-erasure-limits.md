# KB-03 · Erasure: what the bank must keep

Owner: DPO of Company Bank SA (invented). Version 1.1. Audience: privacy desk, DPO.

## The right to be forgotten has limits
The bank must keep some data even after a valid erasure request:
- records of transactions and of customer identification, for 5 years after the end of the
  business relationship (anti-money-laundering law);
- data needed for an open dispute, a court case or a debt collection.

## What the privacy desk does
Erase what the bank does not need to keep, such as marketing preferences and old contact details.
Tell the requester what is kept, why, and until when. Never promise a full deletion at intake.

## Sensitive documents
Health documents sent with a credit application are sensitive data. Their erasure is always decided
by the DPO (see policy section 5).
