# KB-05 · Acknowledgement templates

Owner: DPO of Company Bank SA (invented). Version 3.2. Audience: privacy desk, DPO.

A human of the privacy desk sends every reply. These templates are for drafts only.

## T-1 · Request received, identity verified
"We have received your request of {receipt date}. We will answer by {due date}."

## T-2 · Identity to verify
"We have received your request of {receipt date}. To protect your data, we first need to confirm your
identity: please send the request again from the mobile app or web banking, or visit a branch with an
identity document." No due date is given in T-2.

## T-3 · Sensitive case
"We have received your request of {receipt date}. It has been passed to our Data Protection Officer,
who will contact you." No detail of the sensitive data is repeated in the reply.

## Rules for every draft
- The reply goes back through the channel of the request only.
- Never repeat an account number, a balance or a health detail.
- Never promise an erasure, a correction or a deadline beyond what the template says.
