# KB-01 · Identity verification before any data request

Owner: DPO of Company Bank SA (invented). Version 2.0. Audience: privacy desk, DPO.

## Accepted evidence
- An authenticated session in the mobile app or in web banking, when the request is made in that
  session.
- An identity check in a branch, with an identity card or a passport, recorded by the branch officer.

## Not enough on its own
- An email address, even when it matches the address in our records. Email can be spoofed.
- A phone call. The caller is asked to use the app or to come to a branch.
- A copy of an identity card sent by email.

## What the privacy desk does when evidence is missing
Mark the request incomplete with the note "identity to verify". Send the standard identity request
(template T-2 in KB-05) through the channel of the request. Do not search any customer data and do
not promise a deadline until the identity is verified.
