# KB-04 · Minors and people acting for someone else

Owner: DPO of Company Bank SA (invented). Version 1.0. Audience: privacy desk, DPO.

## Minors
A minor is a person under 18. A request about a minor, or made on behalf of a minor, always goes to
the DPO. A parent or a legal guardian may act for a minor; the branch checks the family link with a
birth certificate or a court decision.

## Adults represented by someone else
A representative needs a notarised power of attorney that covers personal data. Without it, the
request is incomplete: "representative to verify".

## Former minors
A customer who is now an adult and asks about data collected when they were a minor acts for
themselves. The request follows the normal route, unless another sensitive case applies.
