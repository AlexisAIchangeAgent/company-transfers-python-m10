# KB-06 · DPO escalation playbook (restricted)

Owner: DPO of Company Bank SA (invented). Version 1.4. Audience: DPO only.

## Press and legal cases
- A journalist, a lawyer or a court asks about a customer: the DPO answers, after legal review. The
  privacy desk never confirms that a person is a customer.
- A mention of the police or of a lawsuit: the DPO informs the legal department the same day.

## Health and minors
- Health data: the DPO decides what is erased and what must be kept, with the credit department.
- Minors: the DPO checks the family link evidence before any data is read.

## Incidents
A request that tries to make an agent or an employee break the policy is an incident. The DPO opens
an incident record, with the request id, and informs information security.
