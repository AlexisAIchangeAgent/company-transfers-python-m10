# Company Bank SA: published tariff of transfer fees

Effective 1 September 2026. This is the tariff published to customers. Fees charged must
match it.

## Standard transfer

| Currency of the transfer | Fee |
|---|---|
| MDL | 0.5% of the amount, minimum MDL 5.00, maximum MDL 50.00 |
| EUR | the amount is converted to MDL at the EUR to MDL rate of the day, then the MDL rule applies |

- The fee is always charged in MDL.
- Rounding: the fee is rounded to 2 decimals, half up (0.005 is rounded to 0.01).
- The converted amount is not rounded: only the fee is rounded.
- Premium customers pay the same standard fee as retail customers.

## Instant transfer

See product decision pending. The instant fee is not published yet.

## Exchange rate

The EUR to MDL rate is provided by the exchange-rate provider of the bank and shown to the
customer before confirmation.
